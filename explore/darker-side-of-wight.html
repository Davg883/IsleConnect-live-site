The story

What happened here

Peter J. Murray writes the island's legends and darker corners using real settings and real history — which means the places in the book are places you can actually stand in.

Each location carries a short reading from the passage set there. You hear it where it happened.

The book keeps working after the last page. That is the whole idea.
