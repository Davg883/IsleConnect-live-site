About

We put stories back where they happened.

Built in Ryde. Tested in the real world.

IsleConnect brings Ryde's stories to life — helping people discover the history, places and businesses around them.

A fort with no interpretation is a wall. A frontage on Union Street is a frontage, unless someone tells you it is not the one that opened. The material is almost always already there — in an archive, in an engraving, or in the head of someone who has spent thirty years finding it out. What is usually missing is a way for a visitor to meet it at the moment they are standing in front of the place.

That is the whole job.

Ryde is where we are proving the model. The same approach can later bring books, attractions and destinations to life.

Why you can trust it

Real stories. Real places. Human checked.

We use source-linked local knowledge and work with the people who know the story or place. AI helps us research and build these stories faster, but people remain responsible for what gets published.

  • Sources checked
  • Human reviewed
  • Rights respected
Who we are

Historians, artists, venues and the people who keep local knowledge

IsleConnect is being developed in Ryde by David Grannum, bringing together practical business experience, AI production, local research and a growing network of people who understand the Island's places and stories.

We work with historians, artists, venues, authors and community organisations. They retain authority over their knowledge and material; IsleConnect helps turn it into an accessible public experience.

Artificial intelligence supports research, visualisation and production, but it does not decide what is published. Sources, rights and interpretations remain subject to human review.
